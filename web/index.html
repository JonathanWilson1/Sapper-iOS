PROTOTYPE 0.1

FIELD MANUAL

Two fingers scroll and pinch to zoom.

One finger marks earth to dig (yellow hatching). Miners walk out of the Miners' Hut and dig it — they can only reach earth that touches open space. Drag over marked earth to unmark it. Rock is slow. Gold ore pays ◆. Build more huts (ROOM) for more miners.

Wide caverns cave in. Undercut a rock and it drops in one piece.

GENERATORs earn ◆. Keep them 30+ cells apart or they share output. Shallow ones pay most but sit under the shells.

THE GLOW: gas shells and buried munitions from the last war leaked deep into the earth. The deeper a generator sits, the more the Glow saps it.

Tap a GENERATOR to overcharge it: double output for 20s, but sparks start fires that burn props.

Gold ore is worth ◆5 near the top, up to ◆15 deep down, with rare ◆40 nuggets.

UPGRADE (in the tray) buys upgrades: shovels, drill bits, dynamos, plating and more.

Tap your DRILL to bore 20 cells straight down for ◆10, mining any ore it passes. The deep half of the map is rich in gold.

Unbraced tunnel ceilings crumble. Drop a PROP into a tunnel — it fits itself wall to wall.

Sand pours. Water floods. CONCRETE soaks up shellfire — tap it, then draw with one finger.

PUMP drains floods. MG NEST cuts down raiders.

ROOMS: a Field Hospital slowly repairs nearby generators, a Mine Head makes nearby ore pay 50% more, and a Magazine pays ◆40 every wave — but blows up if it's hit. A Listening Post hears enemy tunnellers.

ENEMY TUNNELLERS dig toward your generators to plant mines. Their tunnels are hidden — a listening post reveals them. Dig into one to stop it.

DAILY CHALLENGE: everyone gets the same map and attack order each day. Compare scores with friends.

Drag items up out of the tray to place them. Pinch to zoom.

Lose every GENERATOR and the line falls. Survive as long as you can.
